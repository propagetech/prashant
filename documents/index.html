Preview versus paid copy

A preview is the on-screen RTC, mutation status, encumbrance search result, khata extract, or survey sketch the portal shows before checkout. Screenshot it or use the browser Print dialog and Save as PDF.

A paid copy is a digitally signed EC, certified registered extract, or signed i-RTC the portal sells. Do not buy those unless the owner asks. If bought later, put them in the certified folder, not with previews.

Rules

  • Use official .gov.in portals only. If a deep link has moved, start from the department home page.
  • Sign in with a citizen account that belongs to the business, not a personal login you cannot hand over.
  • Do not use unofficial deed-download sites, Telegram sellers, or agent files of unknown origin.
  • Do not automate this. No scrapers, bots, or anything that skips login, CAPTCHA, or payment.
  • Do not paste survey numbers, owner names, deed numbers, or Drive links into the public listing form.
  • Keep Drive sharing Restricted to named people. Never "Anyone with the link".

What you need from the owner

Write these only in the listing's private 00-index.txt. Never on the website.

  • District, taluk, hobli / village, or BBMP ward
  • Survey number and hissa, or PID / khata for municipal land
  • Sub-registrar office, if known
  • Whether the land is rural (Bhoomi) or municipal (e-Aasthi / e-Khata / e-Swathu)
  • Any scans the owner already holds (those go in owner-supplied)

Recommended pack

Rural / agricultural (Bhoomi). RTC (Pahani) preview. Mutation preview if shown. Kaveri encumbrance search or index. Survey sketch or tippan if Bhoomojini shows it. Tax receipt if a village portal shows it.

Bengaluru municipal / BBMP. e-Aasthi or e-Khata extract. Property-tax view if shown. Kaveri encumbrance search or index. Layout or planning text only from an official BDA, BIAAPA, or BMRDA page if the parcel is in a notified layout.

If a portal has no free view, skip it and write not on portal YYYY-MM-DD in the index file.

How to capture

  1. Open only an official site from the table.
  2. Search with the district / village / survey or PID the owner gave you.
  3. When the record appears, stop before any payment or certified-copy checkout.
  4. Screenshot the record pane, or Print and Save as PDF.
  5. Survey numbers on that screen belong in Drive only. Do not copy them into the listing form.
  6. Upload to the matching listing folder the same day.

Bhoomi. Open RTC / Pahani. Select district, taluk, village, then survey number. Capture the RTC. Capture mutation history if it is on a sibling screen. If i-RTC asks for a fee, stop and note it as paid.

Kaveri. Sign in with the business citizen account if required. Use the official property or Online EC search. Capture the result list or index before any digitally signed download that needs payment. A search hit that names the SRO and year is enough for the preview pack.

Khata and tax. BBMP limits: e-Aasthi. Gram panchayat: e-Swathu. Capture the extract or Form 9 / Form 11 view shown without a paid certified copy.

Survey sketch. On Bhoomojini, open the citizen sketch / tippan / RTC-sketch service if it is listed. Capture the drawing preview. If the service is office-only that week, write that in the index file.

Google Drive layout

Create one private parent folder owned by the business. One folder per public property ID.

Properties / Listings / BLR-PLT-01 / 00-index.txt

01-previews · 02-certified · 03-owner-supplied · 04-ready-to-share

File names: YYYY-MM-DD_portal_doctype_BLR-PLT-01.png. Example: 2026-09-13_bhoomi_rtc_BLR-PLT-01.png.

The index file should list the public ID, village or ward, survey or PID (private), portals checked, date, what was captured, what was missing, and who captured it.

After a buyer enquiry

  1. Open the demand desk, Recent leads, type enquiry.
  2. Confirm the person and the property ID.
  3. Copy only the files you are willing to share into 04-ready-to-share. Watermark if the owner wants that.
  4. Share that subfolder view-only to the buyer email. Do not share the whole listing folder.
  5. Do not put the Drive URL on the public listing.

Other states

If a later parcel is outside Karnataka, use that state's official IGR and land-records portals only. Same preview-only rule, same Drive folder shape, same ban on public survey numbers.

Do not put on the website

Survey numbers, deed numbers, owner names, identity numbers, raw title PDFs, or any Google Drive URL. Public pages keep the reference ID and a non-sensitive summary. Buyers use Contact.
